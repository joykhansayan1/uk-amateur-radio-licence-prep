Privacy Policy

UK Amateur Radio Licence Prep · Last updated: 11 November 2026

MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (“we”, “us”) provides the UK Amateur Radio Licence Prep app for iPhone, iPad and Android (on Google Play: “UK Amateur Radio Prep”) (the “App”). This policy explains how we handle information.

Summary

The App is designed to be offline-first. We do not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Study progress is stored on your device.

Information we process

  • On-device study data — answers, streaks, bookmarks, exam date, daily goals and similar preferences stored in on-device storage (e.g. UserDefaults on iPhone and iPad, or the App’s private storage (SharedPreferences) on Android).
  • Optional notifications — if you enable a daily reminder, iOS or Android schedules a local notification on your device. We do not send push notifications from our servers.
  • Purchases — subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple via StoreKit (iPhone, iPad) or by Google via Google Play Billing (Android). We do not receive your full payment card details.

What we do not do

  • No third-party ad networks
  • No third-party analytics trackers embedded in the App
  • No sale of personal information

Data retention

On-device data remains until you delete the App or reset progress. Purchase records are retained by Apple or Google according to Apple’s or Google’s privacy policies.

Children

The App is a general education tool. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not provide personal information if you are not permitted to use the App under applicable law.
